The Apartment Walkthrough

You're touring a sunny Brooklyn studio. The listing photos were perfect. The agent is smiling. You have one visit before you sign a 12-month lease.

Explore the apartment freely and click on anything you'd want to inspect. Every real check you find gets added to your personal viewing checklist. Find all 24 to finish.

  • Click anything suspicious — areas worth checking light up when you hover them.
  • Walk through doors to reach other rooms.
  • Check your pockets — some tests need things you already carry. Smell the place. On your way out, check the hallway.
  • Stuck? The game nudges you after a while, or press Hint (costs points).
